Application Name: GeoGame

Application Description: A guessing game where it gives the user fun facts about a place, 
country, or region, and the user has to guess what the name of that place is. 

Audience: Students who have an intrest in facts about different parts of the world. 
This is also an educational game for kids.

Vision Statement: For students and general kids who love facts and tidbits about the world. 
GeoGame is an application where kids (or anyone) can play to not only cure boredom, but also learn 
more about the world they live in. Unlike many of the geogaphy games out there on the internet, 
this game challenges the player to connect facts to its respective place.
